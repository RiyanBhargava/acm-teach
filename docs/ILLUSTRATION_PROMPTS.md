## training-inference.png

Use case: scientific-educational.
Asset type: a premium educational illustration embedded in an AI engineering learning guide.
Create a polished wide 3:2 illustration comparing TRAINING and INFERENCE, designed like an excellent illustrated textbook, not a generic flowchart. Warm off-white background, restrained teal, indigo, and amber colors, fine crisp lines, soft dimensional paper-cut details, generous whitespace. No robots or human brains. Two equally sized visual scenes with clear left-to-right arrows.
Left panel heading exactly "TRAINING". A collection of varied data pages flows into a stylized model represented as a network of connected adjustable dials. A few dials are visibly being adjusted, and the output is a completed model tile. Short caption exactly "Learn patterns by updating parameters".
Right panel heading exactly "INFERENCE". A single request card flows into the completed model tile with stable, unchanged dials, then out to an answer card showing simple code-like lines. Short caption exactly "Use learned parameters to produce an output".
Use only those four specified text elements, perfectly legible. Use the same model tile motif in both halves to make the relationship obvious. These are conceptual teaching metaphors, not literal circuitry. No logos, no watermark, no title banner, no tiny explanatory text. Beautiful, calm, professional, and easy to understand on a documentation website.

## rag-context.png

Use case: scientific-educational.
Asset type: polished conceptual illustration for a detailed AI engineering documentation page.
Create a beautiful wide 3:2 textbook illustration explaining Retrieval-Augmented Generation as an open-book reference process, not retraining. Warm off-white paper background, restrained teal, indigo, amber palette, sophisticated cut-paper / softly dimensional editorial style, crisp shapes and generous whitespace. No robots, no human brains, no decorative meaningless circuitry.
Composition: three left-to-right stages with clear arrows. Left: a tidy library of varied document cards, only two relevant passages highlighted in amber; a magnifying glass selectively extracts just these two passages. Center: the two extracted passage cards plus a small question card are arranged together on an open reading desk feeding into a compact model tile (an abstract stable network, no changing weights). Right: a clean answer card with two clearly visible small source-reference tabs matching the two original document colors. Visually distinguish the full collection from the small selected evidence. No arrow from the document library directly to the answer.
Exactly three stage headings: "RETRIEVE", "ADD CONTEXT", "GENERATE".
Exactly one bottom caption: "Relevant evidence goes into the prompt".
Do not put any other words, numbers, fake paragraphs, watermarks, or logos in the image. Represent document content with subtle horizontal marks. Educational clarity and beautiful composition are more important than ornament.
